# Tour Operations

## Physical Visitor Flow

1. Guests pay for tickets.
2. Guests pass the entrance chain.
3. Staff gives them the mini-map.
4. Children receive the Mylotopi Sketch, colored pencils, and a small treat.
5. When QR signage is produced, place the single QR target near the start of the route.
6. Visitors scan the QR target once, then use the on-page section navigation as they follow the route.
